A Living Sanctuary Where Young Women Flourish

Nestled on 24 tranquil acres in Benin City, our campus offers an inspiring blend of classical collegiate architecture, state-of-the-art academic centers, and nurturing boarding life.

Campus Statistics

24 Serene Acres

Solar-powered, 24/7 security perimeter, resident chaplaincy, fully equipped science wing, and recreational athletics fields.

Hostel & Community Life

A Home Away from Home

Our boarding environment is carefully managed by maternal Housemistresses and Reverend Sisters to foster sisterhood, hygiene, and independence.

🏠

Serene Hostels

En-suite boarding rooms equipped with comfortable individual study desks, secure wardrobes, solar hot water, and 24/7 power backup.

🥗

Nutritious Dining Hall

Balanced, wholesome three-course meals prepared fresh daily under the supervision of professional dieticians and catering officers.

🩺

24/7 Medical Clinic

Dedicated on-campus infirmary with resident registered nursing staff, quarantine wards, and ambulance tie-up with University of Benin Teaching Hospital.

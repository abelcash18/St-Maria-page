A Venerable Legacy of Faith, Intellect & Character

Since 1962, St Maria Goretti Girls' Grammar School has stood as an enduring pillar of feminine academic distinction and Catholic character formation in Nigeria.

Motto of Goretti

Veritas, Gratia, Excellentia

Truth in conduct, Grace in spirit, and Uncompromising Excellence in all human endeavours.

Our Origins

Founded on Vision, Nurtured by Sacrifice

Established in 1962 by the Sisters of Charity under the auspices of the Catholic Diocese, St. Maria Goretti Girls' Grammar School was founded on a singular conviction: that educating young women transforms nations.

Named after Saint Maria Goretti — an embodiment of courage, purity of heart, and spiritual strength — the school has evolved over six decades from an initial intake of 45 pioneer students into one of West Africa's most revered girls' boarding institutions.

Here, girls are taught to think deeply, speak persuasively, pray sincerely, and lead with empathy.

Our Vision

To Form Women Who Transform the World

To be a globally recognized center of academic excellence and holistic Catholic formation, nurturing young women who possess exceptional intellect, profound faith, and unwavering moral courage.

Our Mission

Intellect, Virtue and Sisterhood

To provide a safe, disciplined, and technologically enriched learning sanctuary where each girl discovers her unique God-given talents, develops a lifelong love of scholarship, and commits to selfless service.

Safe & Nurturing Sanctuary

Safeguarding & Student Well-Being

At St Maria Goretti, child protection and safeguarding are non-negotiable priorities. Our campus operates under strict pastoral guidelines ensuring zero tolerance for bullying, harassment, or negligence.

  • 24/7 Security personnel and perimeter surveillance
  • Fully-staffed modern medical clinic with resident registered nurses
  • Dedicated School Guidance & Adolescent Counselling department
  • Resident Housemistresses providing maternal mentorship around the clock

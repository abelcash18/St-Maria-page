Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries from prospective families regarding boarding life, academics, and exams.

Are non-Catholic students accepted at St Maria Goretti?

Yes. While St Maria Goretti is proudly Catholic in ethos, tradition, and spiritual daily practice, we warmly welcome students of all Christian denominations and diverse faiths who are willing to respect our school code of conduct and moral standards.

Can day students enroll, or is boarding compulsory?

Full boarding is strongly recommended for holistic character formation, academic study prep, and sisterhood bonding. However, day student enrollment is available for families residing in Benin City who can guarantee punctual arrival for 7:30 AM assembly.

What are the security measures protecting students?

Our 24-acre campus is fully perimeter-walled with 24/7 armed security guards, solar-powered lighting, electronic visitor registration, and resident Reverend Sisters and Housemistresses living within each boarding hostel quadrangle.

How does the school prepare students for international university admissions?

Our Academic Guidance Directorate runs SAT and IELTS preparatory clinics, Model UN competitions, and personal essay workshops. Our alumnae currently study at top collegiate institutions in the UK, Canada, the United States, and across Africa.

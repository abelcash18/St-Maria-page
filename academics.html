Scholarly Rigour, Innovation & Distinction

We provide a dual curriculum designed to stimulate inquisitive intellects, build analytical reasoning, and prepare young women for the world's most prestigious collegiate institutions.

Distinction Record

100% WASSCE Pass Rate

Over 94% of our graduating class achieve A1–B3 distinctions across Mathematics, English, Sciences, and Humanities annually.

Foundational Level

Junior Secondary School (JSS 1 – JSS 3)

The junior years at Goretti cultivate curiosity, strong study discipline, computational logic, and bilingual competence. Each student is exposed to a broad spectrum of disciplines before subject specialization in senior secondary.

  • Core Sciences: Basic Science, Basic Technology, Mathematics & ICT
  • Languages: English Studies, French (Bilingual immersion), Literature
  • Humanities & Arts: Social Studies, Civic Education, Cultural & Creative Arts, Music
  • Vocational & Practical: Agricultural Science, Business Studies & Home Economics
  • Moral Instruction: Catholic Ethics, Character Formation & Civics
Culminates in:

Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with comprehensive career diagnostic evaluation.

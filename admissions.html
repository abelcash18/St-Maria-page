Step-by-Step Guide

The Admissions Journey

A transparent, merit-based selection process structured to identify intellectual curiosity and virtuous character.

1

Application

Submit the online registration form below or obtain the hardcopy admission packet from the Registry. Upload passport photo and academic records.

2

Entrance Exam

Candidates sit for assessments in English Language, Mathematics, and General Aptitude on Saturday, 14th March 2026.

3

Interview

Shortlisted candidates and their parents attend an interview with the Principal, Reverend Sisters, and senior academic faculty.

4

Enrollment

Successful candidates receive formal letters of admission, house allocation, boarding supply list, and orientation dates.

Required Documents

Application Checklist

  • Completed Application Form (Online or Hardcopy)
  • Official Birth Certificate or statutory declaration of age
  • Two recent passport-sized color photographs (red background)
  • Last 2 years of continuous academic assessment reports / Primary 5 & 6 results
  • Letter of moral recommendation from candidate's parish priest or school principal
  • Medical fitness certificate (prior to boarding resumption)

Financial Transparency

Tuition Structure & Sisterhood Scholarships

As a Catholic mission institution, St Maria Goretti maintains competitive, subsidised fee structures without compromising our commitment to world-class faculty, modern laboratories, and premium boarding nutrition.

Sisterhood Endowment Fund: Merit-based awards are granted to candidates scoring in the top 5th percentile of the Entrance Examination. Catholic diocesan need-based tuition waivers are also available for families demonstrating financial hardship.
